## Defining Emotional Intelligence
Emotional intelligence is the ability to do three things.
- Being able to understand emotions
- Regulate your own emotions and
- Use your ability to understand emotions to manage and facilitate your relationships with other people

### This skill is one that is critical to interpersonal relationships for several reasons.
- it determines how you approach other people. 
- How you are able to communicate with other people. 
- How likely you are to being annoyed by other people’s actions or inactions.

# Four Pillars of Emotional Intelligence 
- Self Awareness
- Self Regulation
- Social Awareness
- Relationship Management

## Self-Awareness
- It is your ability to **check into your own emotional state at any given moment.** 
	- Feelings have far more nuances than just good or bad. When you know exactly what you are feeling, you will be able to understand yourself better, recognizing your motivations and why you are doing what you are doing.
- It is also the ability **to be aware of your own abilities accurately.** 
	- You know what you are capable of and what you struggle with. So you will know what you need to work on and what you are capable of.
- It is also the ability **to be self-confident.**
	- When you know your abilities and capabilities you will be able to trust yourself better to do something. If you can trust yourself to do something, you come across as more trustworthy to others.

**Keywords**: *"Emotional self awareness", "Accurate self assessment", "Self confidence"*

## Self-Regulation
- When you are able to use self-regulation, **you are able to manage your own emotions.**
	- With self-control, you are able to make sure that your emotions are always expressed in a method that is effective and appropriate.
- You are able to **Control Yourself**
	- Those who are skilled at self-regulation tend to be **achievement-oriented and self-motivated.** This means that they are able to work toward their goals with their own initiative. When you are willing to be self-motivated, you show that you are willing to work through setbacks like procrastination and not being motivated.
- It's also **the ability to be transparent**
	- You are open to anything and you say what you believe even if that is less popular opinion. That confidence brings a sense of trustworthiness. When you are trustworthy, other people are going to naturally feel like you are more approachable.
- The ability to control your own emotions, also brings with it **the ability to become adaptable**
	- you know that change happens sometimes, and you are willing and able to cope with it. You are able to manage your own expectations, even when they do not come into fruition. Thus you have multiple plans and even if those plans fails, you are optimistic enough to adapt mid way. 

**Keywords**: *"Emotional self control", "Achievement-oriented", "Self-motivated", "Transparent", "Optimistic"*

## Social Awareness
- This is **the ability to understand other people**. 
	- Social awareness comes with 3 major skills
		- The ability to **empathize**
			- Empathy itself is the ability to connect with other people in a way that allows for the acknowledgment and understanding of their feelings. It is critical in relating to other people. You need to be able to acknowledge other people’s emotions if you wish to be able to understand people’s emotions, and *you are not likely to be able to acknowledge the emotions of other people if you do not first understand your own feelings.*
		- The ability of **organizational awareness**
			- Organizational awareness refers to your ability to manage how you explain yourself to others. It is effectively your ability to make sure that you are always speaking at a level that is readily understood by other people. *You are able to ensure that the comprehension levels of those around you always line up with being just right.*
		- Developing a **service orientation.**
			- service orientation refers to just how willing you are to help other people. It involves being willing to contribute to the efforts while also showing that you are happily willing to help guide your group to your desired goal. (leading from the frontline). When you use this, *you are able to make sure that you listen effectively as well.*

**Keywords**: *"Empathy", "Organizational Awareness", "Service Orientation"*

## Relationship Management
- This particular pillar refers to the ability to manage relationships and influence other people.
	- In order to develop relationship management, however, you must first develop all of the other pillars. Relationship management requires you to be intricately aware of your own emotions and behaviors, as well as able to control them. It requires you to have a solid understanding of other people as well. 
	- The skills in relationship management include being able to:
		- Inspire leadership 
		- Influence others 
		- Manage conflict 
		- Trigger change 
		- Develop others and
		- Be skilled in teamwork
