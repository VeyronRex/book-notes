# What is Psychology?
> Psychology is the scientific study of the mind and behavior.

#### To change the way that people think and act. Psychology achieves this by having four main goals: To describe, explain, predict & change

- Describe
	- The first goal is to describe behaviors and thought processes. if you want to be able to tell how someone is going to behave, you would look at several instances that show exactly how they are going to behave.
- Explain
	- Explaining is the second goal. Usually, this goal involves understanding what happened, it looks at the description of what has happened in the describe stage and then begins to come up with several theories that may or may not support it. These theories are meant to come up with whatever the explanation is for that particular behavior.
- Predict
	- During this stage, the explanations created in the previous step are taken and tested. If they fail to meet expectations, they are removed from the list, and they will try to come up with something else.
- Change
	- Finally, once you have been able to describe, explain, and predict the behaviors, you can then begin to understand how to influence change in other people.


## The study of psychology is broken down into five distinct perspectives
- The biological perspective
- The psychodynamic perspective
- The behavioral perspective
- The cognitive perspective
- The humanistic perspective

Each Perspective focuses on completely different part of the mind.
